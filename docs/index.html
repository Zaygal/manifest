Amounts are in USDC. On Arc one USDC is 18 decimal places, not the 6 that ERC-20 USDC uses - this page handles that for you.

Contract address

This is the address of the Manifest contract, not your wallet. It is where a batch gets sent and where receipts are read from, so the app cannot do either without one.

If you have not deployed it yet there is nothing to paste: deploying is one transaction on Arc, about 0.009 USDC, and it happens on the deploy page. That page hands you the address when the transaction lands, and saves it here for you.

Read a receipt

Paste any transaction hash from Arc. The amounts are read from the chain's own Transfer log, so this works for any USDC payment - not only ones that went through Manifest.

Where the numbers come from

On Arc every USDC movement - including a bare transfer with no calldata - emits a Transfer event at the system emitter 0xffff...FfE, because Arc ships EIP-7708 early. On Ethereum the same transfer emits nothing at all, which is why the person who was paid normally has to take the payer's word for it. Here they do not.
